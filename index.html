How to use the planner

Build your base in 3D, then paste the code into ARK's Template Hammer. Your design saves in this browser as you go.

Building

  • Pick a piece from the tabs above the 3D view: Structure, Doors, Defense, Utility or Custom.
  • Click the ground to lay foundations, and drag to paint a whole row.
  • Click near the edge of a foundation to add a wall there. Click the top of your walls to start the next floor.
  • Turrets go wherever you click: a floor stands them up, the top of a wall puts them on top, and the side of a wall hangs them sideways.
  • Pieces need something holding them up, like in game. A green preview can go there; a red one can't, and the bar at the bottom says why.
  • Snap points, like in ARK: aim at a piece and dots show where the one you're holding can snap to it (the big white dot is the one it'll use). Press Q for the next snap point (Shift+Q goes back) and E to turn turrets and other free pieces. A flat pillar aimed at a doorframe snaps at right angles to it, and at another pillar it carries on straight or turns. Untick ARK snapping above the 3D view to aim freely instead.

Tools

Places the piece you picked. Clicking something that's already there never removes it.

Click a piece to select it, Shift-click to add more. Click and hold, then drag a box: everything inside it gets selected, on every floor that's showing, even pieces hidden behind others. Drag to turn the view. In any tool, right-drag also turns it, scroll zooms and middle-drag pans.
Walk · G
Walk through your base in first person and build as you go: click places the hotbar piece at the crosshair, right-click or X removes what it's pointing at. F flies up through the floors, B switches building off if you only want to look around, Esc leaves.
Hotbar · 1–0
Shows along the bottom while you walk. Press a number key to pick that piece. Press I (or click Edit) and then a slot to choose what goes on it. Your hotbar is saved in this browser.
Controls
The keyboard button above the 3D view lets you change any key, the camera and zoom speed, and invert the mouse. Your keys are saved in this browser.
View size
Drag the bar under the 3D view to make it taller or shorter (double-click it to reset). Wide view hides the side panels, Full screen fills your screen, and Faster graphics helps on slower computers.
Pieces

Building faster

Select some pieces and a card appears with more options:

  • Turn and Mirror flip a section around; turrets keep facing out.
  • Copy picks the section up so you can drop a copy anywhere else.
  • Move (T) picks the section itself up: point where it goes and click. R turns it and M mirrors it while you hold it, Esc puts it back.
  • Whole build (Build tab) turns, mirrors or moves everything at once.
  • Import a template (Create tab): paste a Template Hammer export and click Add to my build. You hold the whole import like a paste, turn it with R, and click to drop it onto what you've built, on whatever floor you point at. Its foundations, walls, doorframes and ceilings become normal planner pieces you can build on; everything else (turrets, mod pieces, skins and paint) is kept exactly. Replace my build starts fresh from the import instead.
  • Repeat up stacks copies on top, which is the quickest way to build a tall tower.
  • Turret wall generator (Create tab) builds a straight wall of doorframes with turrets on it: choose its length and height, how far apart the turrets are, posts or thatch, and what goes on top. The turrets all go on the front.
  • Tower generator (side panel) builds a whole tower from a few choices, with or without the 2 × 2 core, and lowers the height by itself to stay inside your turret cap and one template.
  • Sideways turrets on double doorframes (Build settings) can go on the frame's posts, where the doorframe holds them, or in the middle of the opening with a thatch wall behind to swap out later. Switch the ones already placed moves existing ones over.
  • Turret fill puts turrets on every selected wall, hanging outside, hanging inside or on top. The mixed patterns go Tek, heavy, Tek, heavy along each face, so attackers get hit by both kinds equally.

Before you place

  • Check placement marks in red every piece that won't snap in game: flat pillars that don't chain back to a pillar lying on a doorframe, railings with nothing under them, turrets hanging in an open doorframe or standing on nothing.
  • Compare with what placed: after placing, export what got built and paste it in. Missing pieces light up red, extra ones show as blue boxes.
  • Learn from your exports: place a few pieces by hand the way they really work, export them, and the planner copies those exact positions for every piece like it.
  • Turret coverage colours the ground by how many turrets can hit each spot.
  • The Pieces panel shows your turret count against your server's cap and the piece count against the 2,000 limit, plus the materials it all needs.

Getting it into ARK

  1. Click Copy template code at the top of the page.
  2. In game, open the Template Hammer's import and paste the code.
  3. Place the template where you want the base.
  4. The Template Hammer takes at most 2,000 pieces per template. Bigger builds show a Copy part 1, Copy part 2… box instead: place part 1, then each next part on top of the one below.
  5. If you hung turrets sideways, each one has a thatch wall behind it. Swap those for metal doorframes once the turrets are up.

Good to know

  • CS turrets come from the Cyber's Structures mod, so the server needs it. Place one sideways CS turret to test before building a whole tower of them.
  • Tesla coils need the Bob's Tall Tales DLC.
  • Check your server's turret limit before building big towers. The Pieces panel counts every turret for you.
  • Designs are saved in this browser only. Copy the template code somewhere safe to keep a backup.

About

ASA Base Planner is a free, fan-made tool for planning ARK: Survival Ascended PvP bases and turning them into Template Hammer code.

What it does

You build in 3D with the same pieces you use in game: foundations, walls, doorframes, ceilings, pillars and turrets, including turrets hung sideways on walls. The planner writes the Template Hammer code for you, with the right heights and rotations, so the base goes down in game the way you drew it.

Your designs

Everything you build is saved in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded or shared unless you copy the template code and send it yourself.
